# Third-party materials

The root MIT license applies to original Voboost work. It does not relicense external libraries, their modifications, original copyright notices, or identified OEM materials. Preserve the applicable upstream notices when distributing those components. Build and test dependencies retain their own licenses even when they are absent from a release binary.

- third_party/lsplant at a96c7978a9fe578e0e265cc8e45cb117fa8250a3 retains LGPLv3; its own notices and the full GPLv3 must accompany relevant distribution. The implant/include/lsplant.hpp upstream copy, while present, retains LSPlant terms.
- Nested dex_builder at ac7fb2230954ee311808bad469b0db501f31bfb8 is LGPLv3 following upstream PR #5, "Change license to LGPLv3", merged on 2022-01-16 as 9d202f44c90489ef4759328a3f55a56effdc9105 (https://github.com/LSPosed/DexBuilder/pull/5). The relicensing commit is an ancestor of the pinned revision and its LGPLv3 root LICENSE is byte-identical to the pinned file. Stale AGPLv3-or-later headers in dex_builder.cc and include/dex_builder.h, AOSP Apache-2.0 notices and other original notices remain preserved; these retained headers do not undo the explicit upstream LGPL relicensing.
- Nested parallel_hashmap at 0cd57d29a959256ed66b2afdd1009928fc625d09 retains Apache-2.0, Gregory Popovitch and Abseil notices, plus file-specific upstream notices.
- third_party/libsepol at c4882b81f186bc131f79ad0b28808bbeef6fc678 retains LGPL-2.1-or-later and file-specific notices. Patches to LGPL libraries retain their applicable upstream terms.
- Dobby retains Apache-2.0. The armv7 prebuilt has separate Maven provenance in prebuilt/armeabi-v7a/libdobby.provenance.json; its exact source commit is not established by the source-build gitlink.
- yyjson retains MIT with its own copyright notice. LLVM libc++ and compiler runtime retain Apache-2.0 with LLVM exceptions. Rust crates retain the licenses pinned by Cargo.lock.
- TweetNaCl version 20140427 is described by its authors as a self-contained public-domain C library (https://tweetnacl.cr.yp.to/). Both third_party/tweetnacl.h and selinux/tweetnacl.h exactly match https://tweetnacl.cr.yp.to/20140427/tweetnacl.h (SHA256 43f29ad721d9927b747b0100ab4160c119e7bb180c7c98a66e4bf79d31244287). third_party/tweetnacl.c differs from the official 20140427 source only in whitespace; selinux/tweetnacl.c is locally modified. These files retain their TweetNaCl origin and are not assigned the Voboost copyright. Exact upstream/local digests are recorded in installer src/build/license-provenance.json.
- Android linker fixtures, OEM SELinux policy and external OEM inputs retain their original rights. The root MIT grant does not assert permission to redistribute OEM inputs.

Pinned source preparation and patch hashes are defined by tools/prepare-native-third-party-source.ts, tools/prepare-libsepol-source.ts and tools/native-build-source.ts. Build instructions are in README.md and Makefile. Source is available through the pinned submodules and patches; a release must supply its complete corresponding source and the materials needed for applicable library modification/relinking requirements. The ability to install and execute a permitted modified build under the product trust model remains a separate unverified release gate. Do not infer that release obligation is fulfilled by a source link or by dlopen alone.

Packaged original notices are maintained in voboost-installer/src-tauri/resources/licenses/ and synchronized into voboost-app/src/main/license-assets/licenses/. They include full GPLv3/AGPLv3 texts and preserved nested source notices without changing upstream licenses.

Logo assets sourced from voboost-logo are outside this repository’s MIT grant and retain the license supplied with those assets. The logo licensing decision is separate from the current code migration.
